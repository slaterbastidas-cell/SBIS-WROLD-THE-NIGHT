# Aethermoor — Macro Blockout

Nine enormous island-scale regions. Reference area: ~450 km² per principal region. Godot scale: 1 unit = 1 metre. Detailed terrain will use chunks and LOD.

1. Villa Eclipse — northern mountains
2. La Antigua Ciudadela — northeastern monumental region
3. Cleopatt — eastern ancient region
4. Valle Desierto — southeastern region
5. Refugio de los Caídos — lower vertical realm
6. Isla de la Paz — southwestern region
7. La Biblioteca — western knowledge region
8. El Cosmos — northwestern anomalous region
9. Castillo del Cosmos — central nexus

The 12 km conceptual reference is not the physical world diameter; nine ~450 km² regions require a larger coordinate space.
